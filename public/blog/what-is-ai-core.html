What is AI CORE and how it helps you

AI CORE is your personal AI avatar. It is more than a chat: it remembers your conversations and projects, keeps your tasks, and works with Google Calendar, Docs, Gmail, GitHub and video.

Each person has their own avatar and their own data. Nothing is shared between accounts.
